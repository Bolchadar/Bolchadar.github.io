How to Use

• 🙏 Prayed For — Click after your team has prayed for this request

• ✔ Answered — Click when God answers the prayer

• 💬 Follow-Up — Opens WhatsApp with a personalized message for that person. You can edit it before sending.

• Edit — Edit any details of the prayer request (name, phone, country, request, status, notes)

• Share — Share the prayer request with your prayer team via WhatsApp, Facebook, Telegram, or copy it

• Delete — Permanently remove a prayer request

• Follow-up notes are saved automatically so you can track what was sent.
